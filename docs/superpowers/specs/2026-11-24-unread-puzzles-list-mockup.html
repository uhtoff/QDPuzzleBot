Unread puzzles list

The list with last-message previews, folded until you open it. Looking at a chat no longer has to mean leaving your sheet: the panel can show one puzzle while the tab shows another, and a bar at the top gets you to either. Example data throughout.

What the mockup assumes

  1. Ctrl/⌘-click rather than right-click or Shift-click. It is what both browsers already use for "open this in a tab", and middle-click comes with it. Right-click can be taken over in the panel, but it removes the browser's own menu (copy, inspect) and nobody would guess it. Shift-click means "new window" in both browsers. The ↗ on each row covers anyone who doesn't know the shortcut.
  2. The peek bar only shows when the chat and the tab differ. "Back to" carries a count if your own puzzle got messages while you were away. While you peek, your own puzzle can appear in the list like any other.
  3. Folded strip still shows the totals and pulses when something new arrives, so folding it doesn't hide activity.
  4. "Mark all read" sits in a footer under the rows, with the pager. It clears every page, not just the five showing, and leaves an Undo for a few seconds because one stray click would otherwise wipe your triage list. It only shows when the list is open, so it can't be hit from the folded strip.
  5. A solve gets a green row. Tick in place of the count, the answer in place of the last message, and it stays until you look at it or mark all read. After that the puzzle is gone from the list for good: later chat in a solved puzzle does not bring it back. The folded strip gains a green "solved" chip so a solve is visible without opening the list, and the header shows the answer when the chat you are in is solved.
  6. Most recent message first, and a new message returns the list to page 1.
  7. The list covers every unsolved puzzle in the hunt, plus general. Today the extension only counts unread for puzzles you have a tab open on, so this is the part that reaches beyond the panel.
